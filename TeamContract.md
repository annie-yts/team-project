# Team Contract

## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during CSC207. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete our individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class team activities, and our course project.

We treat this as a living agreement: we will revisit it if something isn't working, and we may revise it at any point with the agreement of all team members.

---

## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* **Platform:** We will use our **Instagram group chat** for all day-to-day communication and coordination outside of class.

* **Response time:** Each teammate agrees to read and respond to messages **within 5 hours** when a response is needed (with reasonable allowance for sleep and class time).

* **What to notify the team about:** A teammate will message the group as soon as they know that they:
  - are at risk of missing a deadline or finishing their share of a task late;
  - have to miss a lecture, lab, or scheduled team meeting;
  - are blocked or stuck on a task and need help;
  - have a change in availability (e.g., a heavy week from other courses) that affects the team.

* **Respectful and inclusive behaviour:** We expect everyone to:
  - actively listen to each teammate's ideas and consider them genuinely before responding;
  - give everyone a chance to contribute, and make space for quieter members;
  - critique ideas, not people, keeping feedback constructive and assuming good intent;
  - be punctual and prepared for meetings, labs, and lectures.

---

### Meetings and Attendance

* We will attend the lectures and labs where team activities and project work take place, since a significant portion of our grade (in-class activities, the team component of the two-stage tests, and project work) happens during scheduled class time.
* We will hold a regular check-in **at least once per week**, using our weekly lab/tutorial time, to review progress, surface blockers, and plan next steps.
* If a member must miss a meeting, they will notify the team in advance and catch up on decisions afterward.

### Git Workflow

* No one pushes directly to `main`. All project work is done on a **feature branch** and merged through a **pull request**.
* Every pull request should be **reviewed by at least one other teammate** before it is merged, and the author responds to review feedback promptly. Discussion tied to specific code happens on the PR so decisions are recorded where the work lives.
* We pull the latest `main` before starting new work and resolve merge conflicts locally rather than pushing over a teammate's changes.

### Work Quality Standards

* We aim for code that passes **Checkstyle** and follows the conventions covered in the course before opening a PR.
* We write clear, reasonably small commits with meaningful messages so the history is easy to follow.

### Individual Roles

* We will divide the project into areas and assign an **owner** for each, so responsibilities are clear.
* Each owner is responsible for coordinating work in their area and keeping the team updated on its progress.
* *(Roles to be assigned by the team — e.g., area owners, meeting note-taker, GitHub/PR coordinator.)*

---

## Decision Making

* We will make decisions by **consensus** through discussion whenever possible.
* If consensus cannot be reached in a reasonable time, we will decide by **majority vote**.
* If a vote is tied, we will discuss the trade-offs once more; if still deadlocked, the decision defers to the **owner of that area** of the project.
* Significant decisions will be recorded (in the group chat or on the repo) so everyone can refer back to them.

---

## Conflict Resolution

* We will address concerns **early**, and **privately with the person involved first**, before bringing them to the whole group.
* When a disagreement arises, each person gets a chance to explain their side while the others listen without interrupting, and we work toward a **compromise** the whole team can accept.
* If we're unsure how to settle something, we will **revisit this contract** and the norms we agreed to here.

---

## Accountability

Each team member is responsible for:

* completing their **fair share of the work on time**, so others aren't left waiting or forced to cover last minute;
* **communicating early** when they are at risk of falling behind, so the team can adjust;
* **seeking help** from teammates, TAs, or instructors when stuck, rather than staying silent;
* **reviewing teammates' pull requests promptly** to keep the workflow moving.
